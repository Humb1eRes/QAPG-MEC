# Scientific figure contract

Surface: standalone publication figures, Matplotlib, vector PDF/SVG plus PNG previews. No manuscript insertion in this experiment-only revision.

1. Held-out energy under a common backlog budget: three facets for load beta, four prespecified rho values, six retained methods (72 cells). Plot five-seed mean energy with sample-SD intervals for feasible operating points. Hollow crosses mark test backlog failures; absent feasible selection settings are explicitly identified in the caption and full table. The question is minimum observed energy among tested settings satisfying the same budget, not identical realized queue lengths or global optimality. No invented intermediate operating points; line connections are visual guides only.
2. Selection-grid energy/backlog trade-offs: 162 setting-level observations, nine scales per method/load, two selection seeds. Plot mean energy versus mean backlog with one facet per load. Use log axes to retain every point across the full range. Label this selection data, not held-out validation or an independently optimized Pareto frontier.

All plot numbers come from validated raw-run records / derived audit tables. Exact metrics, targets, chosen scales and failures remain available as CSV. Unit of replication is the random seed, not the slot.

Palette: relaxed multi-category, five roots (blue, orange, gold, olive, pink) plus a neutral sixth series. Distinct markers and line styles provide non-color cues. White background, restrained grid. Paper figures carry no unrelated branding. Footprint: 10.8 by 4.3 inches, three horizontal panels, top legend, source/window context below. Inspect PNG exports at full size before handoff. Data provenance and figure hashes are saved in FIGURE_MANIFEST.json.
